# The Entire History of Ancient Egypt

Fully narrated 16:9 animated comedy documentary, about 15 minutes. One narrator throughout; character voices are listed below. All dialogue is comedic dramatisation, not historical quotation. The only real historical words quoted are the Deir el-Medina workers' complaint, 'We are hungry', from the Turin Strike Papyrus.
